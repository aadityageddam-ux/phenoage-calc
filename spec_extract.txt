0028	SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION
0029	THE PHENOAGE ALGORITHM
0030	Source: Levine ME, et al. (2018). "An epigenetic biomarker of aging for lifespan and healthspan." Aging, 10(4):573-591.
0031	Concept: Combines 9 blood biomarkers + chronological age to predict mortality more accurately than age alone. Represents "phenotypic age" capturing biological aging independent of calendar years.
0032	Why It Matters:
0033	• Validated predictor of mortality, healthspan, physical function
0034	• Correlates with epigenetic age but uses accessible clinical labs
0035	• Tracks intervention effectiveness (exercise, diet, medications)
0036	THE 9 BIOMARKERS
0037	1. Albumin (g/dL)
0038	   Clinical meaning: Liver function, nutritional status
0039	   Aging relevance: Declines with age, chronic disease
0040	   Normal range: 3.5-5.5 g/dL
0041	2. Creatinine (mg/dL)
0042	   Clinical meaning: Kidney function
0043	   Aging relevance: Increases with renal aging
0044	   Normal range: 0.7-1.3 mg/dL
0045	3. Glucose (mg/dL)
0046	   Clinical meaning: Metabolic health
0047	   Aging relevance: Dysregulation reflects insulin resistance
0048	   Normal range: 70-100 mg/dL (fasting)
0049	4. C-Reactive Protein (mg/L)
0050	   Clinical meaning: Systemic inflammation
0051	   Aging relevance: Chronic inflammation = hallmark of aging
0052	   Normal range: <3.0 mg/L
0053	5. Lymphocyte Percentage (%)
0054	   Clinical meaning: Immune function
0055	   Aging relevance: Immunosenescence (immune aging)
0056	   Normal range: 20-40%
0057	6. Mean Cell Volume - MCV (fL)
0058	   Clinical meaning: Red blood cell size
0059	   Aging relevance: Reflects nutritional/bone marrow aging
0060	   Normal range: 80-100 fL
0061	7. Red Cell Distribution Width - RDW (%)
0062	   Clinical meaning: RBC size variability
0063	   Aging relevance: Elevated in cardiovascular disease, frailty
0064	   Normal range: 11.5-14.5%
0065	8. Alkaline Phosphatase - ALP (U/L)
0066	   Clinical meaning: Bone/liver enzyme
0067	   Aging relevance: Increases with bone turnover, liver dysfunction
0068	   Normal range: 30-120 U/L
0069	9. White Blood Cell Count - WBC (10³/μL)
0070	   Clinical meaning: Total immune cell count
0071	   Aging relevance: Elevated indicates chronic inflammation
0072	   Normal range: 4.5-11.0 10³/μL
0073	THE MATHEMATICAL FORMULA
0074	Two-step calculation:
0075	STEP 1: Calculate Mortality Score (xb)
0076	xb = -19.907
0077	    - (0.0336 × albumin)
0078	    + (0.0095 × creatinine)
0079	    + (0.1953 × glucose)
0080	    + (0.0954 × natural_log(CRP))
0081	    - (0.0120 × lymphocyte_pct)
0082	    + (0.0268 × MCV)
0083	    + (0.3306 × RDW)
0084	    + (0.00188 × ALP)
0085	    + (0.0554 × WBC)
0086	    + (0.0804 × chronological_age)
0087	CRITICAL NOTES:
0088	• CRP must be natural log transformed (ln, not log10)
0089	• All biomarkers in EXACT units specified
0090	• Age in years (decimals allowed: 35.5)
0091	• Constant -19.907 is intercept from Levine's model
0092	STEP 2: Convert to PhenoAge
0093	PhenoAge = 141.50 + [ln(-0.00553 × ln(1 - e^xb))] / 0.090165
0094	Where:
0095	• e = Euler's number (2.71828...)
0096	• ln = natural logarithm
0097	• Result = phenotypic age in years
0098	INTERPRETATION
0099	Age Acceleration = PhenoAge - Chronological Age
0100	Example 1:
0101	PhenoAge = 45, Chronological = 40
0102	→ Acceleration = +5 years (aging faster)
0103	Example 2:
0104	PhenoAge = 35, Chronological = 40
0105	→ Acceleration = -5 years (aging slower)

0263	PREPROCESSING PIPELINE
0264	STEP 1: Load All Files
0265	Process:
0266	• Load DEMO.XPT using pandas read_sas
0267	• Load all LAB files using pandas read_sas
0268	• Parse MORT file using pandas read_fwf with column positions
0269	• Verify row counts: ~9,000 participants
0270	STEP 2: Merge Files
0271	Process:
0272	• Start with DEMO as base
0273	• Inner join each LAB file on SEQN
0274	• Left join MORT file on SEQN (some missing mortality data is OK)
0275	• Result: ~4,500-5,000 participants with complete biomarkers
0276	STEP 3: Variable Mapping & Unit Conversion
0277	Create standardized columns:
0278	albumin = LBXSAL (already g/dL)
0279	creatinine = LBXSCR (already mg/dL)
0280	glucose = LBXSGL (already mg/dL)
0281	crp = LBXCRP × 10 (CRITICAL: convert mg/dL → mg/L)
0282	lymphocyte_pct = LBXLYPCT (already %)
0283	mcv = LBXMCVSI (already fL)
0284	rdw = LBXRDW (already %)
0285	alp = LBXSAPSI (already U/L)
0286	wbc = LBXWBCSI (already 10³/μL)
0287	age = RIDAGEYR (already years)
0288	STEP 4: Handle Missing Data
0289	Strategy: Drop participants missing ANY biomarker
0290	Rationale: PhenoAge requires all 9 biomarkers
0291	Expected: ~4,500 participants remain
0292	STEP 5: Calculate PhenoAge
0293	For each participant:
0294	1. Calculate mortality score (xb) using formula
0295	2. Convert to PhenoAge using second formula
0296	3. Calculate acceleration = phenoage - age
0297	4. Add columns: phenoage, acceleration, xb
0298	STEP 6: Data Quality Checks
0299	Check 1: Mean PhenoAge within ±5 years of mean age
0300	Check 2: SD of PhenoAge between 15-25 years
0301	Check 3: Mean acceleration within ±2 years of zero
0302	Check 4: No negative PhenoAge values
0303	Check 5: Correlation with age: 0.85-0.95
0304	If checks fail, review implementation.
0305	STEP 7: Cache Processed Data
0306	Format: Apache Parquet
0307	Location: nhanes_cache/nhanes_processed.parquet
0308	Contents: All NHANES variables + calculated PhenoAge
0309	STEP 8: Fast Loading
0310	On subsequent runs:
0311	• Check if cache exists
